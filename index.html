01 / 05 How it works

From pocket. To wrist.

Step 01 — The cradle

Built around the crown.

The cradle's geometry follows the watch — never the other way around. Lepine clears the crown vertically; Savonnette laterally. Both keep it fully accessible.

MaterialVulcanized silicone
FormatLepine / Savonnette
ContactBioceramic-safe
Step 02 — The strap

Tone on tone.

Cradle and strap finished in matched colorways. Eleven adjustment holes per side meet a stainless pin buckle. Sized once, worn anywhere.

FinishHand-finished silicone
Holes11 per side
Buckle316L stainless, brushed
Step 03 — Enjoy

Enjoy your wrist watch.

The cradle holds. The strap does the rest. Sixty days from order to wrist.
